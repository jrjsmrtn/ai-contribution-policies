**Stance: generated content prohibited, AI permitted for research, learning and translation, all use
disclosed.** Void publishes one contribution policy for the whole project, in the repository GitHub
uses for organisation-wide defaults. It *"applies to all repositories and community spaces,
including GitHub, the Void Linux subreddit, and the Void Linux IRC channels."*[^void-contributing]
The package repository restates that its *"global contributing policy"* applies
there.[^void-packages-contributing]

## The rule

> All contributions are expected to be made by humans. AI tools may be used for research and
> learning, but all content in contributions must originate from and be understood by the
> contributor. This includes code, documentation, issues, security reports, pull request
> descriptions, and comments in all Void Linux community spaces.[^void-contributing]

Three more lines complete it: *"ALL usage of AI tools MUST be disclosed."*, *"Do not bring AI review
tools into pull requests."*, and *"Usage of AI-powered human-language translation tools is acceptable
to facilitate contributions across language barriers."*[^void-contributing]

**Two tests, both required.** Content must *originate from* the contributor and be *understood by*
them. Responsibility rules elsewhere in this bundle ask only for the second; Void asks for both, and the
first is the one that does the work.

**Disclosure covers even permitted use.** Research and learning are allowed, and still have to be
disclosed. The policy names no format.

**The ban on review tools reaches past the contributor's own work** to tools that comment on a pull
request. [Django](../projects/django.md) and [Mesa](../projects/mesa.md) also refuse AI review on pull
requests.

The general section above the AI rule gives *"content generated by a tool"* as a case needing
attribution, and offers *"this change was generated using <tool>"* or a `Co-Authored-By:` trailer as
examples.[^void-contributing] Read with the AI section, that attribution route applies to
non-AI tools: for AI, origin is required, not attribution.

## How it was adopted

The policy was proposed on 2026-06-12 as having been developed *"through an internal consensus
process"*. The pull request collected approving reviews from ten maintainers, with its author adding
themselves to the list, and was merged on 2026-07-14 by the project's own merge
tooling.[^void-policy-pr] GitHub therefore shows it as closed, not merged.

## The first enforcement: vouching is not enough

On 2026-09-12 a package maintainer posted a long comparison of build failures under a Go update. Asked
what produced it, they named an LLM and an agent harness. The reply quoted the policy and
said:[^void-go-pr]

> I'm not going to bother wading through a wall of llm-generated text

The contributor answered that the text was *"well understood, and vouched by me and my tools."* The
answer settles the policy's meaning: *"the policy's pretty clear that the text itself shouldn't be
generated, whether you vouch/understand it or not"*.[^void-go-pr]

**That exchange is the difference between this policy and most others in this bundle.** At
[Fedora](fedora.md), [the Open Build Service](../projects/open-build-service.md) or
[Python](../projects/python.md), vouching for and understanding the work is the requirement. At Void
it is necessary and not sufficient, because the rule is about where the text came from.

The contributor opened a pull request disowning their packages the same day, and merged it themselves
on 2026-09-13: 113 packages now name `Orphaned <orphan@voidlinux.org>` as
maintainer.[^void-disown-pr] No other record here documents an enforcement costing a project a
maintainer, and this cost is countable.

## Where it sits

Void reserves every surface: code, prose, issues, reviews and its forums, like [Zig](../projects/zig.md)
and [Perl](../projects/perl.md) in [prose reserved to humans](../mechanisms/prose-reserved-to-humans.md).
Like [Astral](../vendors/astral.md), it publishes the rule once in the organisation's `.github`
repository, so every Void repository inherits it.

## What a contributor must do

Write your own code, documentation, descriptions and comments. You may use AI to research or learn, and
to translate your own words, but disclose any use. Do not run AI review tools on pull requests. Being
able to explain generated work does not make it acceptable.

## Re-verification notes

Read `void-linux/.github/CONTRIBUTING.md`, which governs every repository; `void-packages` points to it
and adds no AI rule of its own. Watch whether a disclosure format is specified, whether the review-tool
ban is extended or relaxed, and whether further enforcement cases appear in `void-packages` pull
requests.
